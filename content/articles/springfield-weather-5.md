**SPRINGFIELD, Va.** — A mild, mostly dry Saturday will give way to a soggy second half of the weekend in Springfield, with forecasters calling for occasional rain across the area Sunday as part of a storm system sweeping up the East Coast.

AccuWeather put Saturday's high at 65 degrees under partly sunny skies, roughly 10 degrees above the seasonal norm for late November. By 10:28 a.m., the temperature at Springfield had climbed to 57 degrees with a light northeast wind of 1 mph and gusts to 3 mph. Skies were expected to turn rather cloudy overnight, with the low dipping to 49.

Sunday brings the sharpest change: cloudy conditions and occasional rain, mainly from the morning through early evening, with a 65% chance of precipitation. The high was forecast at 63 degrees, the low at 49. Rain was expected to taper off Sunday night, with clouds breaking.

The weekend system is one of two coastal storms forecasters say will spread rain, wind and flooding northward along the Eastern Seaboard — a pair of storms that also threatens to snarl Thanksgiving week travel on both coasts.

Relief arrives behind the rain. Monday was forecast to be partly sunny and warmer still, with a high of 67, before a sharp cooldown sets in Tuesday: sunny skies but a high of only 53 and a low of 31. Thanksgiving Day itself — Thursday, Nov. 26 — was forecast to be mostly cloudy with a little rain and a high near 47.

One caveat for anyone planning to spend long hours outdoors Saturday: air quality in Springfield was rated poor, a level considered unhealthy for sensitive groups. AccuWeather advised people who develop symptoms such as difficulty breathing or throat irritation to limit their time outside. Pollen levels were low, though dust and dander were listed as high.

Daylight is running short, with sunrise at 6:57 a.m. and sunset at 4:51 p.m. — 9 hours and 54 minutes of sun.
