**SPRINGFIELD, Va.** — Springfield residents woke Monday to a hard, clear 33 degrees — the coldest start in more than a week — but the day that follows may be the nicest of the workweek: mostly sunny skies climbing to a high near 56, with no rain in sight.

The mercury stood at 33 degrees with calm winds out of the north-northwest at 2 mph as of early morning, and the slow climb begins at once — the temperature was expected to reach 40 by 7 a.m. and 46 by 9 a.m., according to AccuWeather's hourly forecast. By mid-afternoon, the town will sit at a seasonable 56 degrees before skies stay clear tonight and temperatures slide to an overnight low of 36.

The day fits a pattern forecasters have promised all week: a dry stretch of warming weather after a wintry weekend. Air quality is rated fair — generally acceptable for most, though sensitive groups could feel minor symptoms with long exposure — and mold pollen is running high.

Here's how Monday shapes up hour by hour:

With sunrise at 6:52 a.m. and sunset at 4:55 p.m., the region gets just over 10 hours of daylight — but nearly all of it sun-drenched.

The warmup accelerates from here. AccuWeather's 10-day outlook has Springfield reaching 59 Tuesday, 66 Wednesday and 70 by Friday — some 14 degrees above Monday's high — before a chance of rain returns around Thanksgiving eve.

**The bottom line:** bundle up for the morning drive — that 33-degree start will feel like 35 with the wind — but leave the heavy coat at home by lunchtime. The sun sticks around all week.
