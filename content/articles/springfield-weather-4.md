**SPRINGFIELD, Va. (AP)** — A clear, mild November day settled over Springfield on Wednesday, with forecasters calling for plenty of sunshine and a high of 65 degrees.

AccuWeather reported the temperature at 55 degrees at 10:16 a.m., under sunny skies and a light north-northwest wind of 1 mph. Gusts were measured at 3 mph. The real-feel reading was 62 degrees in direct sun and 57 degrees in shade.

The afternoon was expected to warm steadily: 58 degrees by noon, 60 by 1 p.m., 62 by 2 p.m. and the day's high of 65 by 3 p.m., before readings slide back to 62 at 4 p.m. and 57 by 5 p.m. AccuWeather listed no chance of precipitation at any hour of the daylight period.

Skies were forecast to stay clear into Wednesday night, with the temperature falling to a low of 32 degrees — the first freezing reading of the stretch. Readings were projected at 50 degrees by 7 p.m., 46 by 8 p.m. and 41 by 10 p.m.

Springfield gets nine hours and 59 minutes of daylight Wednesday. The sun rose at 6:54 a.m. and was to set at 4:53 p.m., with a waxing gibbous moon rising at 1:26 p.m.

The forecast offered one caveat for those heading outdoors: air quality was rated poor, a level AccuWeather describes as unhealthy for sensitive groups. The service advised reducing time outside for anyone experiencing symptoms such as difficulty breathing or throat irritation.

The mild weather was expected to hold. AccuWeather called for mostly sunny skies and a high of 66 degrees Thursday, then a warm 70 degrees with plenty of sun Friday. High clouds and a high of 64 were forecast Saturday, followed by a 55% chance of afternoon rain Sunday with a high of 61.

Cooler air was on track to arrive early next week, with a high of 63 degrees and a breeze Monday and 53 degrees under partly sunny skies Tuesday.

AccuWeather characterized the stretch as "very nice for November" through late week.
