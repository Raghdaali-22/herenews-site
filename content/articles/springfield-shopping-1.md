**SPRINGFIELD, Va.** — The bargains did not end when Black Friday did. Several of Springfield Town Center's deepest discounts remain in force through the weekend, and shoppers who time it right — early Saturday morning or Sunday at opening — can cash in without circling the parking lot, according to the mall's deal listings and the AccuWeather forecast.

Saturday, Nov. 28, will be breezy in the morning, then partly sunny and not as cool, with a high near 58 degrees after an overnight low of 34 degrees, AccuWeather said. Sunday brings periods of sun and a high around 57 degrees, though occasional rain is possible. The dry, mild stretch breaks Monday, when strong winds are forecast.

## The deals that survive the weekend

Not all Black Friday pricing makes it to Saturday, a review of the mall's deal pages shows:

[White House Black Market's 40% discount](https://www.springfieldtowncenter.com/deal/3588555) is valid through Saturday only, per its listed terms, making Saturday the last full day to claim it. [Hollister's 30%-off-everything sale](https://www.springfieldtowncenter.com/deal/3591981) runs through Sunday. [Foot Locker's up-to-60%-off offer](https://www.springfieldtowncenter.com/deal/3590215), the steepest of the three, expired Friday night. Store-level offers may vary by location, and the mall's [deal listing page](https://www.springfieldtowncenter.com/) flags new "Black Friday and beyond" offers daily.

The weekend also brings extras: a Holiday Market runs in the Grand Court from Nov. 27–29 — the first of four weekend markets through Dec. 20 — and Santa photos continue at the mall through Dec. 24, adding to weekend crowds.

## When to shop for the easiest parking

The mall ran extended Black Friday hours of 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Friday, per Northern Virginia outlets including [Patch](https://patch.com/virginia/vienna/black-friday-hours-2026-when-malls-will-be-open-northern-virginia), before returning to its regular schedule — 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Saturday and 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Sunday, according to the mall's [FAQ page](https://www.springfieldtowncenter.com/faq). Shoppers should verify tenant hours, as anchor and big-box stores often keep their own schedules.

Based on those hours, the parking picture shapes up like this:

* **Best bet: Saturday at opening.**

   Arriving by 10 a.m. puts shoppers in the lot before the midday surge and before Saturday's Small Business Saturday promotions draw the afternoon wave. It also beats the breeze — AccuWeather expects winds to ease as the morning warms from the mid-30s toward the upper 50s.
* **Avoid Saturday, roughly 1 to 5 p.m.**

   That window stacks regular weekend traffic, Small Business Saturday shoppers and Holiday Market browsers on top of one another — the busiest stretch of the weekend.
* **A solid second window: Saturday after 7 p.m.**

   Lots begin to empty in the final two hours before the 9 p.m. close, and temperatures will still be mild.
* **Lightest of all: Sunday at 11 a.m.**

   The Sunday opening hour is traditionally the quietest slot of a Black Friday weekend — and Sunday's periods of sun and near-57-degree warmth make it the most comfortable walk from the far spaces. Shoppers should finish by late afternoon, before any scattered rain moves in.
* **Skip Monday if possible.**

   Strong winds forecast for Monday will make the walk from distant spots the least pleasant of the holiday stretch.
