**SPRINGFIELD, Va. —** After a Thanksgiving of heavy rain and gusty wind, Springfield wakes Friday to a dry, breezy Black Friday.

AccuWeather calls for mostly cloudy skies and a high of 51 degrees, with a steady breeze and gusts near 18 mph. The overnight low is expected to be 43. At 2:40 a.m., the temperature was 53 degrees with a RealFeel of 48, a south wind at 6 mph and gusts to 18 mph. The chance of rain slides from about 20% in the morning to 7% by early afternoon.

The sun rose at 7:04 a.m. and sets at 4:49 p.m.

Forecasters said the improving conditions should make for dry post-holiday travel through the weekend — a welcome turn after Thursday's storm, which knocked out power and disrupted holiday travel across the region.

### Doors open at 9

Springfield Town Center opens at 9 a.m. Friday,  an hour ahead of its usual 10 a.m. start, and stays open until 9 p.m., according to the mall's hours page. Saturday returns to 10 a.m. to 9 p.m., and Sunday runs 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Individual store and restaurant hours vary, and shoppers should check with a specific retailer before making the trip.

The center's Holiday Market also opens Friday and runs through Sunday, the first weekend of a season-long run that continues through Dec. 20. Santa photos are underway in Grand Court through Dec. 24, family pet photos with Santa are offered Mondays through Dec. 14, and a Toys for Tots collection sits near the Santa set in Grand Court through Dec. 13.

Shoppers who want to give while they shop have a second option: Sheehy Auto Stores is collecting new, unwrapped toys through Dec. 11 at its Ford and Subaru stores on Loisdale Road and its Volkswagen store on Backlick Road during regular business hours. The toys go to local children, including teenagers, through community partners.

### Getting there

Two on-site garages serve the center — one near Macy's, reached from Frontier Road, and one near Firestone Complete Auto Care, reached from Loisdale Road. Electric vehicle charging is available near Macy's on the garage's first level, near Target and near LA Fitness.

Valet service operates near Yard House Friday through Sunday, though hours vary. Overnight and commuter parking are not permitted.

For transit riders, the Franconia-Springfield Metro station on the Blue Line is the nearest rail link. Walkers can reach the mall entrance by Dick's Sporting Goods in about 15 minutes; shuttle and bus service is available from the station.

### Advice for residents

* **Watch for leftovers from the storm.**

   Friday's wind is lighter, but standing water, downed branches and debris may still be on secondary roads. Slow down, and never drive around a barricade or through flooded pavement.
* **Treat dark traffic signals as a four-way stop.**

   Power may still be out in pockets after Thursday's outages, and intersections without working lights are among the most dangerous spots on the road.
* **Give utility crews room.**

   Line trucks and tree crews will be working through the day; move over a lane when you can.
* **Don't warm up an unattended car.**

   Cold-morning idling with the keys in the ignition is an invitation for theft.
* **Shop local first if you can.**

   Small businesses around Springfield and the mall's independently owned kiosks and restaurants see less of the Black Friday crush — and the traffic.
* **Secure deliveries.**

   If you're shopping online instead, porch piracy spikes this time of year; use tracking alerts or a pickup point.

### Advice for visitors and shoppers

* **Arrive early, and have a parking plan.**

   Garage space fills fastest near the anchor stores. Note which garage you used and snap a photo of the level marker.
* **Expect company on the roads after dark.**

   Sunset is 4:49 p.m., and the busiest shopping hours run into the evening.
* **Keep purchases out of sight.**

   Lock bags in the trunk before you leave one store for the next, and park in well-lit areas.
* **Set a meeting spot.**

   Cell service gets sluggish in a packed mall; pick a landmark in advance, particularly if you're shopping with children.
* **Know the amenities.**

   The children's play area is by Macy's on the lower level, and nursing stations are marked on the mall map. Complimentary wheelchair and scooter rentals are not available, so bring your own mobility device if you need one. Only licensed service animals are permitted inside.
* **Confirm before you commit.**

   Doorbuster hours vary by tenant, and some retailers limit quantities. Check a store's own hours and policies rather than relying on the mall's posted schedule.
* **Give yourself extra time to leave.**

   The 9 p.m. close funnels traffic into the garages at once; lingering over dinner at one of the center's restaurants often beats idling in line.

### The weekend ahead

Saturday brings partly sunny skies, a high of 59 and a low of 34, with a 3% chance of rain — the best outdoor window of the weekend. Sunday is partly sunny at 57 with scattered showers and a breeze early, then a better chance of rain returns Saturday, Dec. 5.
