SPRINGFIELD, Va. — Sunshine and light wind made Tuesday afternoon the best stretch of the day in Springfield, with the temperature at 58 degrees shortly after 1 p.m. and a forecast high near 60, according to AccuWeather.

The weather service described it as "a nice afternoon for outdoor activities," with no chance of rain at any hour through the evening. Winds were blowing from the west at about 2 mph, with gusts to 4 mph.

The mild reading came with a caveat in the sun. AccuWeather put the RealFeel temperature at 65 degrees in direct sunlight but 59 degrees in shade, a 6-degree spread that reflected how much the low November sun matters this time of year. Daylight runs 10 hours and 1 minute, with sunrise at 6:53 a.m. and sunset at 4:54 p.m.

Temperatures begin falling quickly after the 3 p.m. peak. The forecast shows 53 degrees by 5 p.m., 49 by 6 p.m. and 43 by 8 p.m., sliding into the upper 30s by 10 p.m. The overnight low is expected to reach 32 under clear skies.

Wednesday and Thursday bring more of the same, with highs of 66 and 67, and Friday is expected to hit 70 and turn warm for mid-November. AccuWeather summarized the pattern as "dry and warming up this week." The next mention of precipitation in the 10-day outlook comes Sunday, when there is a 30% chance of afternoon rain.

Air quality was rated fair, generally acceptable for most people though sensitive groups may notice minor symptoms with long-term exposure. The dust and dander index was listed as high, while tree, grass, ragweed and mold pollen levels were low.

### What to plan around

* **Dress for two seasons.**

   A light jacket or extra layer pays off after 5 p.m., when the temperature drops more than a degree every 10 minutes for a stretch and lands in the 40s by dinnertime.
* **Walk, rake or run early.**

   The window between 1 p.m. and 4 p.m. is the warmest and brightest of the day, with no rain in the forecast and only a light breeze.
* **Watch for frost overnight.**

   A clear sky, nearly calm wind and a low near 32 raise the chance of patchy frost in low-lying yards and open lots. Bring in potted plants or cover tender ones, and plan a few extra minutes Wednesday morning to scrape a windshield.
* **Beat the early sunset.**

   Darkness arrives before 5 p.m., so evening errands, dog walks and bike rides are best done in daylight. Sunset glare can be a factor on westbound roads during the evening commute.
* **Allergy sufferers, look indoors.**

   Outdoor pollen counts are low, but the high dust and dander reading means indoor triggers are the likelier culprit for sniffles.
* **Skip the umbrella.**

   Rain chances stay at or near zero through Saturday, so no need to carry one — but Sunday's 30% afternoon rain chance is worth checking again later in the week.
