SPRINGFIELD, Va. — Clouds hung over Springfield on Sunday as temperatures climbed into the mid-60s, but the calm afternoon was set to give way to a round of showers by evening, according to The Weather Channel's hourly forecast.

Conditions at midday were mostly cloudy with a temperature of 61 degrees, and the mercury was expected to peak at 65 degrees between 3 p.m. and 4 p.m., the forecast showed. Winds stayed light out of the northeast at about 5 mph through the afternoon.

The wet weather was set to arrive around 6 p.m., when the chance of rain jumped to 31%. Showers were likely through the evening, with rain chances peaking at 66% around 9 p.m. as temperatures slid into the upper 50s. Rain was forecast to linger past midnight before tapering off in the early morning hours Monday.

Humidity climbed steadily through the evening, reaching the upper 90s around midnight — a sign of the saturated air moving in with the showers.

Monday looked milder but grayer, with a morning temperature near 52 degrees and an afternoon high of 68 degrees expected around 1 p.m., the forecast showed. Winds picked up out of the northwest at about 9 mph, gusting to 16 mph in the afternoon, though rain chances stayed low at 15% or less.

A sharper change arrived Monday night, as skies cleared and temperatures tumbled. The temperature was forecast to fall to 43 degrees by 3 a.m. Tuesday, with wind chill values dipping to 35 degrees, and a morning low near 38 degrees.

Sunshine returned Tuesday, with sunny skies and temperatures climbing through the 40s by midmorning. Winds were forecast out of the north-northwest at 6 to 9 mph, with gusts up to 17 mph.
