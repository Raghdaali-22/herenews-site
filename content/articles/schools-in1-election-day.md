SPRINGFIELD, Va. (Nov. 2) — Schools across Springfield have been converted into polling places for voters casting ballots in the midterm elections, with doors open from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.

Ilryong Moon, a member of the Fairfax County School Board, said in an email that the occasion matters because voting ensures decisions reflect the experiences and needs of the county's diverse communities — at the local, state and federal levels.

He added that the day is an invitation to reflect on the partnerships that help Fairfax County Public Schools thrive. "Our schools are not just places where students learn during the school day," he said. "They are a gathering place for the broader Fairfax County community."

Fairfax County school facilities host more than 500,000 events a year and welcome millions of visitors, he said. From gymnasiums and athletic fields to other shared spaces, the buildings support learning, recreation and connection for all ages.
