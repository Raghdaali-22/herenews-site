**SPRINGFIELD, VA** — Residents of Springfield should keep the coats handy this weekend. According to AccuWeather's forecast, the next two days will be seasonably cold, breezy, and dry — the tail end of a chilly stretch before a notable warmup arrives next week.

## Saturday, November 14: Cloudy and Breezy

Saturday opened with mostly cloudy skies and a brisk west-northwest wind. As of early afternoon, the temperature stood at **49°F**, with a RealFeel® of 47°F and gusts reaching **18 mph**. The high is expected to top out around **50°F** before clouds begin to break apart this evening, with an overnight low of **42°F** under partly cloudy skies. Precipitation chances are minimal — around 61% cloud cover but essentially no rain expected — and air quality is rated **excellent**, so outdoor plans don't need to be scrapped, just layered for.

The sun sets early this time of year — **4:56 PM** — giving the area just over 10 hours of daylight, so late-afternoon errands will finish under dimming skies.

## Sunday, November 15: Some Sun, Still Cool

Sunday brings a modest improvement: **breezy at times with some sun**, a high near **51°F**, and a colder overnight low of **39°F**. With only a 20% chance of precipitation, the day is dry, though the breeze will keep it feeling sharper than the thermometer suggests. It's a good day for a walk at Lake Accotink or weekend yard work — with a windbreaker.

## The Bigger Picture: A Warmup Is Coming

The weekend chill is the last gasp of a colder pattern. AccuWeather's 10-day outlook shows a steady climb beginning Monday (54°F) and accelerating through the week: **59°F Tuesday, 63°F Wednesday, and 65°F Thursday**, with plenty of sunshine and clear skies each day. By Friday and Saturday of next week, highs reach **67°F and 64°F** respectively — a roughly 15-degree swing from this weekend.

**The takeaway:** enjoy — or endure — the last cool weekend of the stretch. Dry conditions and improving air quality make it perfectly usable, but next week's sunshine and 60s will feel far more forgiving.
